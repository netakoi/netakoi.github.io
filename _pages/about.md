I am currently working as an Apollo 11 Postdoctoral Fellow at Purdue University, IN, US. 

My research aims to achieve "Reliable Autonomy in Complex and Interactive Systems", in which I address several issues raised by complex, high-dimensional modern dynamical systems; and the complicated interactions among multiple agents (including humans). 

1 Structured Modeling of Complex Systems and Interactions
======

Modern dynamical systems often face difficulties in modeling their dynamics and interactions, especially while preserving their mathematical & control-theoretical properties. These challenges may arise from highly nonlinear, high-dimensional dynamics (e.g., humanoid); data-driven or learning-enabled components (e.g., neural networked controller); or simply a lack of analytical models (e.g., human). This thrust aims to develop structured modeling frameworks that address these challenges and provide efficient, reliable representations of complex systems and their interactions.
